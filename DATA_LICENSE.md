Short evidence excerpts support verification; they are not offered as a corpus of freely licensed original prose.
- Event dates, times, prices, addresses, and eligibility facts are provided with official provenance. Public visibility alone is not a blanket redistribution license for third-party material.

## Coordinates

Nassau coordinates were independently replaced before this repository's first publication. `geocodes.json` records US Census address matches and reviewed OpenStreetMap objects. US Census coordinate data is US government public-domain material; OpenStreetMap data is © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under ODbL: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright . The combined structured database uses ODbL accordingly.

Other initial venue points are retained from official venue/event location information and approximate editorial placements. New coordinates must have explicit redistributable provenance. Restricted geocoder results and their raw responses must not be committed.

## Accuracy

This is an independently maintained listing, not an organizer's live booking service. Use `edition.verifiedOn`, per-source review dates, and coverage notes when reusing a snapshot. Confirm changes, eligibility, and registration availability with the original organizer.
